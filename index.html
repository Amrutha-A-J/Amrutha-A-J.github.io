01 / About Me

Get to know me

9+ years experience

Hi, I'm Amrutha Adiyath, a full-stack developer based in Moose Jaw, Saskatchewan. Over the past 9 years, I've built web applications using React, TypeScript, Java, Python, PostgreSQL, and modern cloud technologies. I enjoy solving real-world problems, learning new technologies, and creating software that makes people's lives easier. I also run Pixelmoose, my solo software studio, where I design, build, and support custom websites and applications for small businesses and nonprofits.

Outside of software development, I spend most of my time with my husband and our five rescue cats. I enjoy creating JavaScript and CSS experiments, crafting polished user interfaces, and volunteering with animal rescue organizations by fostering cats until they find their forever homes.
